Captain's log... Stardate -298776.4116575593 on a blue planet... it all started with a chat with a Polish construction worker. Fiber-optic rollout is finally kicking off in our village—and nothing is going according to plan. Right from that very conversation, it turns out only 15 meters of fiber cable on your own property are actually free now. So much for minimally invasive routing across the yard; time to tear up the paving stones after all. Directional boring is apparently only used in exceptional cases.

Work on our property was supposedly starting the very next day, yet not a single worker was in sight. Actual kickoff happened the following Monday at 2:00 PM... with zero prior notice or coordination. Crews just walk onto properties and start ripping up pavers and digging trenches across driveways. Anyone who only notices the crew once they've already started and didn't move their car ahead of time won't be driving off their property for the next few days. EV owners can't reach their home chargers either, with the nearest public charging station about 8 km away—and combustion-engine drivers are equally stranded for their weekly grocery run, including elderly neighbors who rely on rides to shop.

With zero reliable scheduling, planning work-from-home days is impossible too—some people need to let crews into their basements, while others can't concentrate over the racket and would have rather gone into the office.

Evidently, the town didn't coordinate with ambulance services either: medical transport vans ended up bumper-to-bumper against oncoming traffic inside the narrowed village thoroughfare, with no temporary traffic lights in place. Because of the curve at the village entrance, drivers couldn't even see how long the construction zone stretched.

Even today, on day three of the rollout, it's not an alarm clock waking you up, but the sound of shovels scraping right along the house wall.

On the bright side so far: the crew hasn't severed any gas, water, power, phone, or satellite TV lines yet. And credit where it's due—the sheer speed and work ethic of these workers rivals an ant colony.

I'm using my little blog here to document what's happening... because for now, you won't find this story anywhere official. Stuck on a 7 Mbps connection, our village desperately needs this upgrade. Filing a complaint with the town would only cause more delays, and the rollout has already seen plenty of those. True to "don't bite the hand that feeds you," everyone stays quiet and puts up with the lack of communication.

Still, if anyone out there is planning a fiber rollout and happens to stumble across this blog: please, for the sake of the residents, handle it differently.
