Thanks to the solar storms currently sweeping through our solar system, we're getting a once-in-a-lifetime treat: tonight, you can actually spot the northern lights right here in Saxony! Auroras are a breathtaking natural spectacle dancing across the night sky, triggered by solar wind streaming out from the sun. When those charged particles slam into Earth's atmosphere, they collide with gas molecules, energizing the atoms and making them glow.

I've already finished editing the first batch of photos and wanted to get a quick post up.
