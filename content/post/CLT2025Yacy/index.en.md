Soon it will be that time of year again, when crowds of enthusiasts gather in Chemnitz to swap ideas. This year, I'm heading back to the European Capital of Culture (2025) as well. Over two days, people will showcase their projects and passions in the lecture hall building—through booths, workshops, and talks covering topics like VPNs, Hugo websites, and AI.

For many years now, this has been a fixed date on my calendar. I'm usually either in the building running the booth for YaCy or volunteering to help out with the event. This year, I'm back at the YaCy booth.

# YaCy@CLT2025

Once again, we plan to present [YaCy](https://chemnitzer.linux-tage.de/2025/de/programm/beitrag/211) as the Open Source world's search engine. As always, our focus remains on privacy and data sovereignty. What originally hooked me on the project was its censorship resistance, and I've stuck with it for many years now.

## My Plan for CLT2025

I'm trying to get my hands on a RISC-V mainboard before the event. My idea is to run a search engine not only on open software, but increasingly on open hardware too. Since stock and ordering are still a bit hit-or-miss right now, though, I can't make any promises just yet.

## My "Services" for You at CLT2025

Like every year, I'll be bringing a YaCy node to CLT. It will let you crawl your own websites into the YaCy index and make them searchable across the World Wide Web. Fingers crossed it'll be running on RISC-V by then—otherwise, business as usual on x86_64.
