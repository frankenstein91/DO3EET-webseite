I was out and about in Germany again—and not exclusively on Deutsche Bahn trains this time. My journey from home up to Rendsburg included roughly 110 km aboard the IC384 bound for Aarhus. The train's onboard software proudly showed off the rolling stock's origins in glorious retro orange.
{{< imgwebp src="https://do3eet-media.dreamofjapan.de/posts/NOC1/PXL_20231122_134737072.jpg" alt="Seat reservation display" width="800" height="600" >}}

Unfortunately, the weather didn't quite match what I'd hoped for. It rained pretty much nonstop the entire trip. Still, you can have a great time even in the rain.

My first stop was Rendsburg, where I visited the High Bridge spanning both banks of the Kiel Canal (Nord-Ostsee-Kanal). The bridge is a local landmark and looks especially striking lit up at night. Its lighting is actually interactive and changes based on input from visitors down by the bridge. Since I skipped riding the transporter bridge that evening, I only got to tweak the lights on one side.

{{< imgwebp src="https://do3eet-media.dreamofjapan.de/posts/NOC1/PXL_20231122_180531020.LONG_EXPOSURE-01.COVER.jpg" alt="Illumination of the High Bridge in Rendsburg" width="800" height="600" >}}

After checking out the bridge, I strolled into downtown Rendsburg. Plenty of charming shops and restaurants around—so I treated myself to a tasty dinner at a cozy little spot.
