Nagoya, Japan's fourth-largest city, often gets overlooked. Many travelers know it only as a transfer stop between Tokyo and Kyoto. Yet Nagoya has a lot more to offer than just a convenient train station! At least if you believe YouTube and a few travel agencies. And I can back them up. Whenever I'm here, I love visiting the castle and admiring the Science Museum from the outside. With that massive sphere of steel and concrete, it never fails to impress.
