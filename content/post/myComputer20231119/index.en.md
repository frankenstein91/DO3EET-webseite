Today I want to introduce my computer, which I built almost entirely from secondhand parts. First and foremost, this custom rig is the beating heart of my creative workflow, especially when it comes to bringing my personal programming projects to life. There's something genuinely unique, rewarding, purposeful, and inspiring about spec'ing out and assembling a machine tailored to my exact needs.
<!--more-->

Every component choice—from the GPU down to the CPU—reflects my vision of a powerful, rock-solid system that lets me turn ideas into reality without a hitch. And it's not just indispensable for personal tinkering... it's also proven itself an essential partner in my professional work. Whether crunching through complex codebases or testing software stacks, my homemade PC always has my back.

My computer is like a shapeshifting companion in the digital universe! 🚀💻 It slips effortlessly into all kinds of roles and crushes every challenge thrown at it. 🎮🎥 As a gaming rig, it sweeps me away into breathtaking worlds, only to transform seconds later into a powerhouse video-editing suite bringing my creative projects to life. 🎬🖥️ With its wizardry in photo editing, it turns every snapshot into a masterpiece and serves as my #1 creative tool. 📸💡 Wait, there's more—my trusty sidekick also doubles as a virtualization lab, spinning up virtual machines and letting my developer dreams take flight. 👾💻 And as an entertainment hub, it delivers unforgettable moments of pure relaxation and distraction. 🎉🎶 My computer isn't just hardware—it's my digital playground where all my passions come alive and run wild! 🔥✨

# System Specs 🖥️🔍

## Core Components
- **Motherboard:** Supermicro X9DRi-LN4+/X9DR3-LN4+
- **CPU:** Dual Intel(R) Xeon(R) CPU E5-2640 0
- **RAM:** 384GiB Hynix Semiconductor (16GiB DIMMs)
- **GPU:** NVIDIA Corporation GeForce RTX 3060 Lite Hash Rate

## Peripherals
- **USB3:** uPD720201 USB 3.0 Host Controller
- **GPS:** u-blox 7 - GPS/GNSS Receiver
- **WiFi:** Intel Corporation Wireless-AC 9260
- **Audio device:** C-Media Electronics Inc CM8888 [Oxygen Express]
- **Fingerprint Reader:** DigitalPersona, Inc.

## Input Devices
- **Keyboard:** Razer USA, Ltd BlackWidow Tournament Edition Chroma
- **Mouse:** Logitech Inc. MX Master

I intentionally left out the SSDs and hard drives.

I run Arch Linux as my OS and BTRFS as my filesystem. Should I write a post about that sometime?
