The city is famous for its countless monuments, memorials, and museums dedicated to World War II and the dropping of the American atomic bomb. As sad and important as that topic is, I still want to mention an alternative—because there are people who might not be able or willing to engage with something that heavy while on vacation. I checked out that alternative myself today, and I'm thoroughly impressed.

# Mitaki-dera
This gorgeous temple complex is just a short train ride and a few minutes' uphill walk away. Soon enough, you're standing inside a wonderful temple tucked into the forested slopes of Mount Mitaki. You wander through a quiet forest, the sound of a mountain stream accompanying your steps across the grounds.
