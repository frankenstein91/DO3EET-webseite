This year I also made it out to Fuji—specifically to the town of Shin-Fuji. Unfortunately, I didn't get to see the impressive mountain itself this time, though I did walk away with a certificate instead. According to the certificate, Mount Fuji was hiding… because I'm too handsome and he's too shy. So off I went to the Fujisan Hongū Sengen Taisha Shrine instead, to take photos of lanterns and ducks.
