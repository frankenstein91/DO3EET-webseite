Fushimi Castle, once a magnificent landmark near Kyoto, stands abandoned and inaccessible today. What was once a place full of enthusiasm and beauty now resembles a "lost place", hidden from public view and surrounded by caution tape. This state is not only a loss for Japan's cultural diversity, but also a waste of resources. Fushimi Castle has the potential to be a vibrant place of community, education, and inspiration.
<!--more-->

The replica of the castle impresses with its traditional architecture and imposing appearance. Fushimi Castle could be a magnet for tourists from all over the world and boost the regional economy. Furthermore, this site could divert some visitors away from Kyoto's crowded city center and help relieve the city.

{{< imgwebp src="https://do3eet-media.dreamofjapan.de/posts/BurgFushimi2024/PXL_20241114_020239699.jpg" alt="Fushimi Castle" width="1400" height="1400" >}}

**Therefore, I urge those responsible to reopen Fushimi Castle to the public!**
